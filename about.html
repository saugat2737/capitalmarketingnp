Company Story

Trust, quality, and long-term relationships at the center of every order.

Capital Marketing & Trading Pvt. Ltd. helps organizations choose corporate gifts that feel useful, well-made, and aligned with their brand reputation.

Chandra Thakur
Founder & CEO

Founder & CEO

Chandra Thakur

With a commitment to dependable service and carefully selected products, Chandra Thakur has built Capital Marketing around a simple belief: corporate gifts should strengthen relationships, not merely fill a procurement list.

The company focuses on quality, clear communication, and practical guidance for organizations ordering at scale. Whether the requirement is a branded pen for a school event, an annual diary for a bank, a recognition trophy for a ceremony, or a complete employee welcome kit, the team supports clients with product knowledge and reliable execution.

Every client relationship is treated with patience and professionalism, because the final gift carries the reputation of the organization that gives it.

Mission
